The short version

  • We collect only what is needed to run campus placements: your account, academic details, resume, applications and training records.
  • Placement officers see your data. Companies see your profile and resume only for drives you apply to.
  • We never sell your data and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • You can see, correct, erase or export your data and withdraw consent by emailing {{ PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL }}. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

This Privacy Policy explains how the placement cell of {{ INSTITUTION_NAME }} ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the Campus Placement and Recruitment Portal ("CPMS", "the portal"). It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the DPDP Rules, 2025. For people in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, section 8 explains your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). Please also read our DPDP Act Notice, which lists each item of data and its purpose.

{{ INSTITUTION_NAME }} is the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act and the data controller under the GDPR. We decide why and how your personal data is used in the portal. You are the Data Principal (or "data subject").

4. Who we share it with

  • Placement officers and authorised staff of {{ INSTITUTION_NAME }}, who need your data to run placements. Access is limited by role.
  • Companies and recruiters, but only for a drive you apply to. They receive your name, contact details, academic details, resume and, where relevant, interview results. They do not see your data for drives you did not apply to. We ask companies to use it only for hiring for that drive, to keep it secure and to delete it when they no longer need it.
  • Service providers that host or support the portal (for example hosting and email providers). They act only on our instructions under a contract, as the DPDP Act requires of Data Processors.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. You can ask us for the names of the organisations we have shared your data with (see section 7).

5. Transfers outside India

The portal is operated from India. The DPDP Act allows personal data to be transferred outside India except to countries the Government of India restricts. If a company or provider outside India receives your data, we share only what is needed. If you are in the EEA or UK, we also use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights (DPDP Act)

As a Data Principal you have the right to:

  • Access information: a summary of the personal data we process and what we do with it, and the names of organisations we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete and update your data. You can edit your phone number and resume yourself on your profile page. For your name, USN, department, CGPA or backlogs, ask the placement office.
  • Erase your data when it is no longer needed for its purpose or you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. This does not affect what we did before. If you withdraw consent to share your profile with companies, you can no longer apply to drives.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us and get an answer.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

How to use your rights: email {{ PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL }} from your registered address. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We aim to reply within 30 days and will always answer a grievance within 90 days at the most. There is no charge. The DPDP Act also lists duties of a Data Principal (section 15), described in our DPDP Act Notice.

8. If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR)

If the GDPR applies to you, the legal bases in section 3 apply, and you also have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data and complete incomplete data;
  • erase your data ("right to be forgotten") in the cases the GDPR lists;
  • restrict processing, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • data portability: receive the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Use the same email contact as in section 7. We will reply within one month, which we may extend by up to two further months for complex requests.

9. Security and breaches

  • Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes, never as plain text.
  • Students and placement officers have separate logins, and every page checks your role before showing data.
  • Forms are protected against cross-site request forgery, and uploaded resumes are limited to PDF, DOC or DOCX files up to 2 MB.
  • In production the portal must be served over HTTPS, and access to the database and uploaded files is limited to authorised staff.
  • We keep access logs so that we can detect and investigate unauthorised access.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we will tell the affected students without delay and report it to the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require, and, if the GDPR applies, to the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where required.

10. Cookies

The portal uses only the cookies it needs to work: a sessionid cookie that keeps you logged in and a csrftoken cookie that protects forms. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept. You can block cookies in your browser, but then you cannot log in.

11. Automated checks

When you open a drive, the portal automatically compares your CGPA, backlogs and department with the drive's published rules and tells you whether you can apply. This is a rule-based check, not profiling. Decisions to shortlist, select or reject you are made by people (placement officers and the company). If you think an eligibility result is wrong, ask the placement office to review it.

12. Under 18s

The portal is for students who are 18 or older. The DPDP Act requires verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before we process the data of anyone under 18, and we may ask for proof. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour and we do not show targeted advertising.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version number and date at the top show the latest update. If a change affects how we use your data in a significant way, we will tell you through the portal and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again.

14. Contact and complaints

Please contact us first at {{ PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL }} or write to the Grievance Officer at {{ INSTITUTION_ADDRESS }}. We will try to resolve your concern promptly.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act, or, if you are in the EEA or UK, to your local data protection supervisory authority.
