About this notice

This is the notice that the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 ("DPDP Rules") require us to give you before we ask for your consent (section 5 of the Act and Rule 3 of the Rules). It says, in plain words, what personal data we collect, why, and how you can exercise your rights or complain. It is a companion to our Privacy Policy.

The Rules phase in their main duties, with most applying from 13 May 2027. We already follow them.

3. Consent and withdrawal

  • When you register you tick separate boxes. Your consent is free, specific, informed and clear: you must tick each box yourself, and none is pre-ticked.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing {{ PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL }} from your registered address. Withdrawal does not affect what we did lawfully before.
  • If you withdraw consent to share your profile with companies, you can no longer apply to drives. If you withdraw consent to all processing, we will close your account and erase your data, except what we must keep by law.
  • We do not currently use a Consent Manager. You may use one registered with the Data Protection Board once they are available.

4. Your rights

Under sections 11 to 14 of the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access information: a summary of the personal data we process about you and what we do with it, and the names of the other organisations we have shared it with.
  • Correction, completion and updating of your data. You can change your phone number and resume yourself on your profile page. For your name, USN, department, CGPA or backlogs, ask the placement office.
  • Erasure of data we no longer need or that you no longer consent to our keeping, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Grievance redressal: a way to raise a complaint with us and get an answer.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

5. How to use your rights

Email {{ PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL }} from your registered email address, and tell us what you want to do. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We aim to reply within 30 days, and we will always respond to a grievance within 90 days at the most.

6. Complaints to the Board

Please raise your concern with us first, using the contact above. If you are not satisfied with our reply, or we do not reply in time, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Your duties

Section 15 of the DPDP Act also gives you duties. Please:

  • follow the law when you use the portal;
  • not pretend to be another person;
  • not hide material information when giving your details, and give only true and verifiable information;
  • not make false or frivolous complaints; and
  • give accurate information when you ask us to correct or erase data.

8. Retention and erasure

We keep your data only as long as the purpose needs it. Account, academic, application and placement records are kept while you are a student and for up to {{ DATA_RETENTION_YEARS }} years after your graduation year. We then erase them or anonymise them. Server and access logs are kept for at least one year because the DPDP Rules require it for security and breach investigation. We keep data longer only when another law requires it.

9. Data breaches

We protect your data with reasonable security safeguards (see the Privacy Policy). If a personal data breach happens, we will tell you without delay, in plain language, what happened, what data is affected, the likely consequences, what we have done, and what you can do. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board as the Rules require, including a detailed report within 72 hours.

10. Children

The portal is for students aged 18 and above. If you are under 18, the DPDP Act requires verifiable consent from your parent or lawful guardian before we process your data. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour and we do not show targeted advertising.

11. Transfers outside India

Your data is processed in India. If a company or provider outside India receives it, we share only what is needed for the drive or service. The DPDP Act allows such transfers except to countries the Government of India has restricted.

12. Language and updates

This notice is in English. You can ask for it in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India by emailing us. We will update this notice if how we use your data changes, and show the new version number and date above. We will ask for your consent again where the law requires.
